Video Desk

Video Desk is a private tool that Liran Friedman runs on the owner’s own computer to prepare and manage the videos of the owner’s own YouTube channel, “fritzzzz-ai”. It is not a public product: there is no sign-up, and it has one user, the channel owner.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads the owner’s finished videos to the owner’s own channel as private drafts.
  • Fills in titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists.
  • Uploads English captions and downloads the automatic Hebrew captions of the owner’s own videos.
  • Reads comments and channel statistics, for the owner to see.

It never publishes or schedules a video, changes its privacy, deletes a video, or replies to or moderates a comment. Publishing is done by the owner in YouTube Studio.
