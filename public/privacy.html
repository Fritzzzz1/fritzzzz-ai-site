Video Desk

Privacy Policy

“Video Desk” is the name of the Google sign-in behind two private tools that Liran Friedman uses for the owner’s channel. This page explains what Google data they use, why, where it is kept, and how you can remove it.

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · About Video Desk · fritzzzz_ai home

Who this is

These tools are run by Liran Friedman (fritzzzz_ai), Kfar Saba, Israel. They are not a public product: one part is used only by Liran, the other only by people Liran personally invites. Questions or complaints about privacy: friedmanliran@gmail.com.

1. Video Desk guest sign-in

Video Desk is Liran’s private dashboard for preparing videos for the YouTube channel “fritzzzz-ai”. Guests invited by Liran sign in with Google to watch drafts and leave comments.

What we receive from Google

Only your email address, and Google’s confirmation that it is verified. Video Desk does not ask guests for contacts, files, a YouTube account or anything else.

How it is used

  • To check your address against Liran’s list of invited people. If it is not on the list, you are not let in.
  • To keep you signed in. One cookie holds your address, signed so it cannot be forged, for 7 days. A second, short-lived cookie exists only during sign-in. There are no other cookies, no analytics and no advertising.
  • To show who wrote what. Comments, notes and ideas you leave in Video Desk are saved with the part of your address before the “@”.

How long it is kept

The sign-in cookie expires after 7 days, or when you sign out. What you write in Video Desk stays until you ask for it to be removed or Liran removes it.

2. YouTube connection (Liran’s own channel only)

This app uses YouTube API Services. Liran connects it to the owner’s own YouTube channel “fritzzzz-ai”, with the owner’s own Google account, to upload and prepare the owner’s videos. No other person’s YouTube account is connected or used, and guests never reach YouTube data.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner’s finished videos to the owner’s own channel as private drafts. Publishing is done by the owner, in YouTube Studio.
  • Reads and fills in a video’s title, description, tags, category, language, recording date, “made for kids” and “altered content” settings.
  • Sets the video’s thumbnail.
  • Uploads the English caption file, and downloads YouTube’s automatic Hebrew captions of the owner’s own videos, saved on the owner’s computer.
  • Adds the video to one of the channel’s playlists.
  • Reads the comments on the channel’s videos, so the owner knows which ones are waiting for a reply.
  • Reads the channel’s statistics (YouTube Analytics) for a video or for the whole channel, for the owner to see.

What it never does

  • Publish or schedule a video.
  • Change a video’s privacy, or delete a video.
  • Change a video that is already public.
  • Reply to, like, hide, delete or moderate a comment, or write anything about a comment. The owner answers comments on YouTube.

What data it accesses

The app asks for two Google permissions: youtube.force-ssl (manage the owner’s YouTube videos, captions, playlists and comments, as described above) and yt-analytics.readonly (view YouTube Analytics reports, read only).

  • The details listed above for videos on the owner’s channel, the channel’s playlists, and each video’s upload and processing status.
  • The caption tracks of the owner’s videos, including YouTube’s automatic Hebrew captions.
  • Comment threads on the channel’s videos. A comment includes the commenter’s public YouTube name, the text and the time it was posted, as shown publicly on YouTube.
  • Statistics reports from YouTube Analytics for the owner’s channel or one of its videos, read only: views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers and similar channel and video metrics.

What is stored, and for how long

  • The access token that Google issues for the owner’s account, kept only on the owner’s own computer, until the owner disconnects it.
  • A record of each change the app made (what was there before and after), and a copy of any thumbnail or caption file it replaced, so a change can be undone.
  • The downloaded Hebrew caption files and the statistics reports the app read, saved on the owner’s computer.
  • Which comment threads the owner has already been told about, so the owner is not told twice.

This data is kept on the owner’s computer only. Stored YouTube data is deleted or refreshed from YouTube within 30 days, and is deleted when the connection is withdrawn.

AI assistant

Liran works with an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) on the owner’s computer. The assistant reads video details and new comments on the owner’s computer to tell Liran about them. It uses this data for nothing else.

3. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising. No third party serves content or ads in these tools.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Your choices

  • Revoke access. You can remove this app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google’s security settings, also at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Once access is revoked, the app can no longer reach the YouTube account, and the stored access token and the YouTube data listed in section 2 (change records, replaced files, caption files, statistics reports, comment results) are deleted from the owner’s computer.
  • Delete your data. Write to friedmanliran@gmail.com and everything stored about you will be deleted within 7 days. This removes it from these tools only; it does not delete anything held by YouTube or Google.
  • Sign out. Signing out of Video Desk removes the sign-in cookie at once.

5. Google and YouTube terms

The YouTube features of this app use YouTube API Services. By using them, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Changes and contact

If what these tools do with Google data changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top. Questions, requests or complaints: friedmanliran@gmail.com.
